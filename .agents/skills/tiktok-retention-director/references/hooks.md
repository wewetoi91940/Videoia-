# Hook lab — five typed hooks, one honest choice

The hook is the first 0–2 seconds: what the viewer **hears**, **reads** and **sees** before deciding to stay.
On TikTok the first frame also serves as the paused thumbnail, so it must already carry the hook's picture
and text — no fade from black, no logo, no "hi everyone".

General hook doctrine (outcome language, value before evidence) lives in
`../../hyperframes-creative/references/story-spine.md`; the nine explainer hook strategies live in
`../../faceless-explainer/references/story-design.md` § Hook strategy. This file adds the five-hook lab, the
honesty test and the short-form constraints.

## Anatomy of one hook

| Part | Constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Spoken line | ≤ 12 words in French, ≤ 10 in English (≈ 2–2.5 s of speech). One idea. |
| On-screen text | ≤ 6 words, readable in 1 s, inside the safe area; not a copy of the spoken line when a shorter label hits harder |
| First-frame visual | the most striking true image of the subject (archive, map, object, number); it moves within 0.5 s |
| Sound | the first audible thing is the voice or a meaningful sound, never silence |

## The five types

Write one hook of each type, from the *core surprise* found in Step 1.

| Type | Mechanism | Formula (adapt, don't copy) | Example — subject: the 1910 Paris flood |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Curiosity** | opens a precise gap the video closes | "Why did …?" / "What … actually …?" | « Pourquoi circulait-on en barque dans les rues de Paris ? » |
| **Contradiction** | sets a belief against a verified fact | "Everyone thinks X. In fact, Y." | « La Seine ne déborde jamais vraiment ? En 1910, elle a pris Paris. » |
| **Surprising fact** | states the strongest number or detail plainly | "<number/detail> — and it's true." | « En 1910, l'eau est montée à 8,62 mètres dans Paris. » |
| **Suspense** | starts in the middle of the action, with a clock | "<Date, place>: <threat>. <time> to …" | « 28 janvier 1910. La Seine monte encore. Paris retient son souffle. » |
| **Unexpected consequence** | shows an effect whose cause the video explains | "Because of <cause>, <surprising effect>." | « À cause d'une rivière, Paris a vécu des semaines sans métro. » |

Example numbers in this table are illustrations of the format; every real hook's facts come from the
project's `SOURCES.md`.

## Honesty test (every hook, before choosing)

A hook fails — rewrite or drop it — if any answer is "no":

1. **True** — every claim in it is in `SOURCES.md` with status `established` (or is worded as a hypothesis).
2. **Paid off** — the video answers the question or shows the promised thing, explicitly, before the end.
3. **Proportionate** — the tone matches the stakes (no "shocking", "insane", "they don't want you to know"
   for an ordinary fact; no false urgency; no exaggerated number or rounded-up figure).
4. **Not bait** — the first frame and the on-screen text show the real subject, not an unrelated image.
5. **Understandable alone** — someone who reads the text without sound understands what the video is about.

## Choosing — rubric

Score each surviving hook 0–2 per line, keep the highest total; on a tie, prefer the hook whose payoff
arrives last (it carries retention to the end).

| Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Surprise for this audience | they did not know it | half-known | common knowledge |
| Clarity in 2 s | instantly clear | needs the second sentence | needs context |
| Visual strength of the first frame | a real, striking image exists | a good diagram/type treatment | generic |
| Payoff strength | the end delivers a real reveal | a partial answer | only a summary |
| Fit with audience and tone | exact | acceptable | off |

Record the five hooks, the honesty results, the scores and the reason for the choice in `DIRECTION.md`
§ Hooks. Keep the runner-up: it is a ready variant for an A/B re-upload.

## Anti-patterns

- Opening with a greeting, the channel name, "in this video", or a definition.
- A question the video never answers, or answers only with "nobody knows" without saying so up front.
- A hook built on the least verified fact because it is the most spectacular.
- Text on the first frame that sits under TikTok's top bar or right rail (see `vertical-direction.md`).
- A first frame that is black, a logo, or a fade-in (the paused thumbnail is then empty).
