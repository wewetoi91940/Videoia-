# DIRECTION — crue-1910-v2

Strategy line: **This video makes young adults curious about history feel January 1910 from the inside — the clocks
stop, the water takes the city, Paris holds together — and leaves them with a mark they can still find on the quays.**
Destination: TikTok · 1080×1920 · 52.8 s · français

V2 of `videos/crue-1910-tiktok/` after the request « plus d'émotion… un peu plus nouveau… dans la mode de 2026 ».
Same facts and sources; what changed is the emotional arc and the form.

## What changed from V1, and why

| V1 | V2 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrator at a distance, past tense (« La Seine a arrêté… ») | « POV » in the present, tutoiement (« Imagine : Paris, 21 janvier 1910 ») | immersion: the viewer lives the day instead of hearing about it |
| One mood (sober suspense), one music bed | an emotion per frame (9 emotions), a score that follows it | a flat arc tires; a contrast (fear → solidarity) is what people remember and share |
| Voice at one speed (0.92) | 0.85 – 0.95 per line | slower on the sinistrés and the peak, faster in the rising water |
| Sentence captions on a dark box | word-by-word captions, 1–3 words, the word said in amber | the 2025–2026 short-form standard; easier to follow without sound |
| Static header titles | titles that enter word by word on the voice | motion tied to the voice, not decoration |
| Archive in a fixed stage | archive as a rounded card over its own blurred copy, two camera speeds | depth (parallax) without inventing anything |
| Same grade everywhere | colour by emotion: cold → night → soft → warm → paper → natural | the arc reads even without sound |
| No people | the gymnase dortoir (empathy), the *Petit Journal* front page and the motto of Paris (solidarity) | emotion comes from real people and a real document |
| Number appears | riser → white flash → count-up → 2-frame shake → impact | one "impact" moment, used once |
| Ends on a credits card | the hook clock comes back and ticks again | loop ending: a replay feels continuous |
